## Project Focus:
A student relies on multiple social media posts to find bar deals around campus. This is cumbersome because social media posts can have more than just deals, making it hard to find them.

Our goal for this project is to fix this issue by creating a central application to aggregate bar deals around campus. We will use an AI to scan through social media posts to check for potential deals. If time permits, the scope of this project can expand to broader businesses beyond bars.
