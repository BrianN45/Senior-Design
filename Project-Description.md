## Project Topic Area
A tool that helps University of Cincinnati students find the best bar deals happening around campus. The system uses AI-powered web scrapers to continuously monitor social media platforms, like Instagram, Twitter/X, and Facebook, where local bars post their daily specials, happy hours, and event promotions. Instead of students manually checking dozens of bar accounts, the AI parses posts, extracts relevant deal information (drink specials, discounts, event times, locations), and organizes it into a simple, searchable feed. The goal is to save students time and money by surfacing the best nearby deals in real time, tailored to the UC campus area.
