## Co-op Work Experience

### Co-op Systems Designer at Grantek Systems Integration
**May 2025 to August 2025 and January 2026 to August 2026**

During my two co-op terms at Grantek, I worked on industrial automation projects. A lot of my work involved testing programs, finding problems, and making changes so the systems worked more reliably. I also helped create screens used to monitor equipment and set up an inventory system for the company.

This job helped me improve my programming, testing, and troubleshooting skills. I also learned how to work with systems that were new to me instead of expecting to understand everything right away. Outside of the technical work, I became more comfortable asking questions, explaining what I was working on, writing instructions, and giving updates to other members of the team.

### Software Engineering Intern at Regal Rexnord
**June 2024 to August 2024**

At Regal Rexnord, I worked on software projects that supported engineering and manufacturing. I automated data tasks, helped improve older programs, and worked with data collected from testing equipment. One of my projects reduced the time needed for a repetitive process by about 85 percent. This showed me that even a fairly simple program can make a noticeable difference when people have to complete the same task every day.

The internship gave me more experience with programming, data analysis, and debugging. It also helped me become more independent and taught me to think about whether a solution is actually useful for the people who will be using it.

### Part-Time Data Analysis Work at Regal Rexnord
**September 2025 to December 2025**

I returned to Regal Rexnord part-time while I was taking classes. I helped with data analysis and testing for a newer gearbox project. Balancing work with school improved my time management and organization. Since I completed much of the work remotely, I also had to communicate clearly and make sure the team knew where I was with each task.
